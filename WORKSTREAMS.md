# Workstreams

- [DM-D.S.S OAuth and EEW forecast](workstreams/dmdss-oauth-and-eew-forecast.md) — first Android integration slice: public-client OAuth authorization-code PKCE, encrypted token persistence/refresh/re-authorization/revocation, contract visibility, resilient socket cleanup, persistent intake/notification diagnostics, bounded read-only `gd.eew` post-event recovery, fully independent audible Forecast (Shindo 3/4) and Warning (Shindo 5− through 7) channels and attention controls, a distinct new alert when an active event escalates into Warning, cold-start restoration of active EEW details/rings/countdown with layout- and geometry-ready focus, a matching Sandbox forecast injector, and a dedicated `eew.forecast` WebSocket layered over the existing single P2PQuake baseline. Other DM-D.S.S classifications remain out of scope.
- [Differentiate city reporting units](workstreams/differentiate-city-reporting-units.md) — all 4,360 audited English names are active in the app through a code-keyed runtime map. Current JSON-only placement research has source-supported facility and/or address data for 2,944 stations, including 2,734 exact addresses; 1,416 remain locality-only.
- [Observed-intensity hierarchy and deep-map UI](workstreams/observed-intensity-hierarchy-and-deep-map-ui.md) — `0.9.84z` fixed sibling JMA-area stacking; the device re-test then exposed untranslated island-area headers and cache-dependent municipality inheritance. `0.9.84aa` adds build-validated English names for all 188 station-backed areas and generates all 1,894 code-keyed municipality parents from the audited source. Regional focus, station details, true-marker labels, and inherited tier colours remain unapproved. Neighborhood boundaries and OSM are excluded.
