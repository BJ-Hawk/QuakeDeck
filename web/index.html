Japan live.
Without the clutter.

QuakeDeck is an independent English-first Android earthquake monitor built around a fast, detailed map of Japan, observed JMA intensities, municipality-level visual Shindo, and a future live DM-D.S.S early-warning stream.

What exists today

A serious map before the serious live feed.

The current Android prototype already displays recent JMA earthquakes, historical events, municipality and prefecture shading by observed Shindo, station observations, progressive labels, and event-aware camera controls.

🗾

Detailed Japan map

Prefecture geometry, progressive city and station labels, deep zoom, historical-event focus, and a lightweight regional context layer.

📍

Municipality-level visual Shindo

At deep zoom, QuakeDeck colours individual municipalities and designated-city wards by their highest observed JMA intensity—not just an entire prefecture.

⚡

DM-D.S.S next

The planned production milestone is browser-based OAuth, reliable WebSocket reception, successive EEW reports, cancellations, and live observations.

Capability probe

Find out what the JQuake-exclusive subscription actually exposes.

The diagnostic page reads contract and socket information, then calls Socket Start once to test QuakeDeck’s earthquake + EEW entitlement. It never opens the returned WebSocket and immediately attempts to close any socket it creates.
