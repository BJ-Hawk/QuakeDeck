Sources, licences, and care.

QuakeDeck is built from public information, open data, and third-party services. This page identifies the sources used by the Android project, what QuakeDeck does with them, and the applicable licence or service terms. The processed resources in QuakeDeck are not official products of their source organisations.

Attribution matters. Processed map and catalogue data may be simplified, quantised, translated, or otherwise adapted for mobile rendering. The source links below are the controlling terms; if a source changes its terms, the source’s current terms prevail.

Geography and official earthquake information

MLIT National Land Numerical Information — N03 administrative areas

Used for: Japan prefecture and administrative geometry, processed into compact map resources.

Terms: MLIT National Land Information terms. The data are available without charge; attribution is required when the data or a processed result is republished. QuakeDeck’s attribution: “National Land Numerical Information (Administrative Area Data), Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism; processed by QuakeDeck.”

Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA)

Used for: reporting-area, EEW warning-area, tsunami-area, municipality/ward GIS geometry; place-name translations; and the published Noto-event material used to reconstruct offline test fixtures.

Licence: Public Data Terms of Use, Version 1.0, unless a particular item carries different rights information. The terms require a source statement and, for adaptations, a statement that the material was processed. QuakeDeck credits JMA and identifies its processed resources as QuakeDeck adaptations. JMA logos and marks are not used.

Live data and public services

P2PQuake API v2 and sandbox

Used for: current and historical earthquake, EEW, and tsunami reports, plus the public replay sandbox.

Terms: P2PQuake secondary-use policy. P2PQuake permits commercial and non-commercial reuse, subject to its stated notices and no-warranty condition. QuakeDeck does not redistribute a verbatim archive of P2PQuake traffic; its bundled Noto fixtures are independently reconstructed for deterministic testing.

DM-D.S.S

Used for: optional browser OAuth capability testing; a live provider integration is planned but not yet the production app data source.

Terms: access is governed by the account holder’s DM-D.S.S contract, OAuth consent, API terms, and technical limits. No DM-D.S.S data, tokens, client secrets, or socket tickets are bundled with QuakeDeck.

JQuake Desktop

Acknowledgement: JQuake is an important reference point and inspiration for QuakeDeck’s exploration of a capable, English-first Android earthquake monitor and the initial DM-D.S.S compatibility research.

Use and rights: no JQuake code, artwork, datasets, account credentials, or service content is bundled, copied, or redistributed by QuakeDeck. This acknowledgement does not imply affiliation, endorsement, or a licence relationship with JQuake.

Nager.Date holiday API

Used for: on-device public-holiday schedule lookups. The app requests yearly holiday data directly from the service and caches it locally; the website does not use this API.

Terms: Nager.Date terms of service. This is a live service integration, not a bundled QuakeDeck dataset; use remains subject to the provider’s current service terms.

Supporting datasets

Natural Earth

Used for: low-resolution regional land context around Japan.

Licence: public domain. Natural Earth permits use and modification without permission; QuakeDeck gives the courtesy credit “Made with Natural Earth.”

iku55/jma_int_stations station catalogue

Used for: a provisional, bundled baseline of seismic-intensity station coordinates, refreshed from the upstream export when available. It helps join observed stations to official JMA regions.

Rights status: the local QuakeDeck checkout records this source but does not retain an explicit upstream licence text. It is therefore marked licence confirmation pending. Before a public production release, QuakeDeck will confirm the upstream reuse permission or replace this provisional catalogue with an official JMA distribution. This transparency note is intentional: a missing licence should never be hand-waved away.
